# DFH - Democracy and Federalism Hub

**Website:** https://dfh.org.ps/

DFH is a joint movement of Israeli and Palestinian young activists, building a federal, practical, and optimistic alternative to today's reality. The movement operates on two fronts: politically, engaging decision-makers to influence and advance policy, and civically, building a grassroots movement that already demonstrates cooperation between the two sides. The movement's roadmap includes three stages: a local alliance of interests, a Palestinian-Israeli federal framework, and a Levantine federation.
